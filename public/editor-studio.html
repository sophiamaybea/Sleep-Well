Poem Verse

After the Diagnosis, My Mother Cooks

She fries onions until they
disappear into the oil, the way
a name disappears
from a hospital form.

The kitchen fills with something
close to ordinary.

pinned: "the word 'ordinary' doing immense work"

Why we pinned this

The restraint. The cooking as displaced grief. Every line earns what it withholds. We want more poems where the wound is structural, not announced.

by R. Adeyemi

On Slowness, or the Problem with the Well-Made Sentence

What we call style is almost always the writer's relationship to time. The sentence that takes the long way around is not being difficult. It is staging the difficulty of the thing it describes. Syntax enacts. Grammar is not neutral.

Flash fiction Fiction

Inheritance

My father left me his silence and a good knife. I use both to prepare fish. The silence I inherited whole; the knife I had to teach myself to hold properly because he never showed me. This is how most things are passed down.

Why this works

102 words. An entire father-son story. The knife doing triple duty. Nothing wasted, nothing sentimental. We want more flash that works like this.

Flash prose Fiction

The Last Visit

She showed me her roses and I said they were beautiful when what I meant was: I am afraid of you dying. She said she was thinking of cutting them back. I said that sounded sensible. We talked about roses for forty minutes. We said nothing that wasn't true.

Why this is us

The whole emotional landscape rendered in the gap between what is said and what is meant. Not subtext as technique — subtext as survival. This is exactly what The Page Gallery is for.

What is The Studio

A space within The Page Gallery where literary journals curate reading boards — not submission calls, but sensibilities. A way of showing, before the writer submits, what the editors are actually drawn to.

How it works

Any journal can create a board. Pin excerpts from submissions, published work, fragments, references, editor's notes. The board tells writers: this is the kind of mind we are.
