## The Summary

The posting flow works. The writing editor is clean. The new features (First Reader, Reading Shelf, The Café with daily questions) all have the right shape — they're simple, fast, and purposeful. The 3-step publishing wizard is a good idea executed almost well.

But the *interaction* layer is thin. I can drop reactions and icons but I can't actually talk to anyone about their writing. I can't see if anyone responded to mine. The social features are mostly one-way gestures into a quiet room. For a platform that promises community, the inability to write even a sentence to another writer about their piece is the most fundamental gap. You've built a space where writers can exist near each other. You haven't yet built a space where they can speak to each other.
